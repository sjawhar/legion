# Source: decision D1 of the plan of dispatch://INFRA-988, which counts what the loop's driver
# reads over a driver day. Every weight below is that read's share of the loop's reads, and the
# seven add up to 99: the loop's remaining 1% is reads of no one shape.
#
# Every row's caller is `loop`, the loop's driver, as routes-2026-10-26-1719Z.txt names its
# callers browser, cli, pod and other. Each row's placeholders resolve against the proposal the
# request landed on, which the step draws in proportion to that proposal's requirement ids
# (run.go -loop-seed, loopseed_export.go).
#
# Format, one route per line:
#   <weight> <caller> <identity: person|agent> GET <path template>
# Placeholders: {key} {project} {part_artifact_id} {part_slug} {review_page_artifact_id}
# {review_index_artifact_id} {search_term} (a proposal name, the target of a requirement query)
# {status_term} (one of the six words a thread's status ends under). Only a loop seed fills the
# last six; a step without one is refused by the placeholder's name.
