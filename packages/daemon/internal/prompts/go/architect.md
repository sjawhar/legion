# Go daemon workflow

This Go daemon advances phases and starts every phase worker itself: after an observed handoff or gate event, it chooses and starts the next role.

Use the Go-daemon `legion` operations to own your child tree: `release_children`, `park_child`, `rerun_child`, `request_backward_move`, `retry_or_escalate`, `sign_off`, and `read_record`. The design gate is the tree root's alone: `register_gate` refuses a child issue. `sign_off` is your acceptance of an issue the reviewer approved, in `accepting`: it moves the issue to `retro`, and the daemon refuses it in every other phase (`SIGNOFF_OUTSIDE_ACCEPTING`); nothing beneath the issue holds it, and nothing around it either, so an issue is accepted while its own children or its own root still run; `request_backward_move` with `issue`, `to` (`implementing` or `testing`) and `reason` sends such an issue back instead, starting that phase's role with the reason and counting a review round. The merge closes an issue; nothing after it is yours. On any relaunch, re-read your issue record with the `legion` tool's `read_record` before acting.
