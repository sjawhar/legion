# Go daemon workflow

This Go daemon advances phases and starts every phase worker itself. Do not choose or start the next phase. When review is complete, end this phase with the `legion` tool: `op: "handoff_complete"` and a `summary`.

Under this daemon, submit `APPROVE` on a clean head even though it carries `.legion/`. This overrides the shared role text, which reserves `APPROVE` for a head with no `.legion/`: the Go daemon has no `.legion/` deletion step before Stage 7, every tester and reviewer handoff writes `.legion/` again, and the handoffs are removed from the default branch after the merge. A `COMMENT` review decides nothing here, so a clean round submitted as `COMMENT` leaves the issue in `reviewing`: approve it, or request changes when a correctness finding stands. A round you complete with no review that ends it is told to your architect, which asks you for the decision: submit the `APPROVE` of the head it names, or the `REQUEST_CHANGES`, then. Your completion is already recorded, so that review ends the round without another completion.

A review ends in `APPROVE` of the head by its SHA or in `REQUEST_CHANGES`; there is no hold. Work that does not change the head, such as resolving another reviewer's or a bot's threads, belongs to retro and the merger and never gates your approval. Anything that would change the head is a `REQUEST_CHANGES`, naming what must change.

The shared role text orders each round: your handoff pushed first, then your review of the head that push made, with an approval waiting for that head's CI verdict to settle. Under this daemon, the `legion` tool's `read_record` for your issue shows both: `pullRequest.head` is the commit you pushed once the push has landed, and `checksVerdict` is the CI verdict settled on it, judged only by the checks the base branch requires: `red` when one of them failed, `green` when every one passed, and absent until the daemon has read the required set or while a required check is cancelled or has no result, which a later settlement of the head decides. A check the base branch does not require never makes it red. Until your handoff head's own CI settles, the verdict is the code head's carried to it. When your own head's CI has finished and `checksVerdict` is still absent, a required check was cancelled there or never reported: approve as the code deserves and complete, and keep that approval. The daemon tells the architect which check it waits on, and the round ends when a later run of that check on your head passes. A red settled on your own handoff head is your round's to decide: the head's code is the code you reviewed, so the daemon sends nothing back for it and waits for your decision. Request changes naming the failing checks. A completion that names no new handoff commit for the round is refused (`HANDOFF_NOT_NEW`).
