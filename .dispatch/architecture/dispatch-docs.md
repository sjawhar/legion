The live document surface: the collaborative websocket room, two-phase settlement that mints block ids before it persists and compares what it rendered, the agent edit operations (`replace`, `delete`, `insert`, `retype`, `move`, `delete_row`, `delete_column`), block tokens and preconditions, and ask and comment anchor refresh.

A room's browser updates go to the store in batches. ygo hands each update to the room's persistence once (`StoreUpdateContext`); the call waits until 50 ms after the room committed the update for the room's queue to fill, cut short when a settlement, Shutdown's drain or any other durability waiter waits on the room, and then stores its own update and the run of the room's queued updates after it, at most 256 updates and 1 MiB, in one transaction with one row per update: the rows the same updates leave stored one at a time. The transaction runs on a connection of the `RoomAppends` pool (8 connections, `application_name=dispatch-room-appends`; the rooms pool that loads documents is `dispatch-rooms`) in about three round trips per batch: the document's session and transaction locks, the prune recovery and the gate in one pipeline, the insert with both notifications, the owed settlement, the pending authors and the commit in a second, and the unlock. A batch takes no second connection while it holds the document's lock, and no holder of a document lock waits for a `RoomAppends` connection (settlements, live writes and the other `withRoomLock` writers keep theirs), so the pool cannot deadlock.

Every write that changes a document's stored content (an append batch, a rebuild, a prune's checkpoint, a deletion) sends two notifications in one statement inside its transaction: `dispatch_doc_changed` with the bare artifact id, and `dispatch_doc_head` with `{r,v,p}`, the document, its head once the transaction commits and the writing process's id. A compaction sends neither. Each process listens on both on the stale-room evictor's one LISTEN connection. A head notification for a document this process holds no room for, or whose room stands at `v`, costs no query; one for this process's own write waits, with no query, until that write's bookkeeping has advanced the room's cursor and compares again; anything else, another process's write, is decided on a goroutine of that room's (a read of the head, then a read under the document's advisory lock before the room is closed), so one room's lock wait never holds the listener. A bare `dispatch_doc_changed` only invalidates the head tracker's entry for the document: every write of this build sends the head notification beside it, so the bare id alone is an older build's write during a roll, which this build's rooms do not follow (their next durable write is refused, `ErrRoomBehindHead`, and they reload).

The head tracker (`head_tracker.go`) is what a process knows of each document's stored history without reading it: per document, a generation every write it hears of moves on and the last head it heard, and an epoch per LISTEN connection, so a reconnect discards everything heard before. This process's own writes are followed from their gate to their end; a shared-pool transaction ends its write through `store.AfterCommit`, before the call that committed returns, so a process reads its own writes at once. A cold read serves its cached rendering with no database read while nothing has written the document since it was read, and a cold socket's preload is checked against the tracked head; otherwise each reads the store. The tracker trusts itself only while its LISTEN connection stands and has delivered something within the last 3 s (`headTrustWindow`), so a half-open connection, up but carrying nothing, which pglisten's idle ping finds only after about 35 s, stops being trusted within 3 s. To keep a healthy idle connection delivering, the evictor also listens on `dispatch_doc_ping` and, while its process holds something the trust serves (a resident room, a cached cold read, a preload or a tracker entry) and its connection has delivered nothing for its quiet window, sends `pg_notify('dispatch_doc_ping', <process id>)` through a shared-pool connection, bounded at a second; a pool too busy to lend one skips the ping, and trust lapses to store reads. The quiet window is a second plus a jitter under a second, drawn afresh after every delivery and every ping, so processes that heard the same ping do not ping together and the first holder to fire resets everyone else's window; the longest gap between pings is under 2 s plus a delivery, inside the trust window. A process holding none of those sends no ping and still counts what it hears as delivery, so a deployment sends about one ping a second while some process holds a room, a cached read, a preload or a tracker entry and no document write is announced, and none otherwise. The bound: a read on one process can lag a commit on another by the notification's delivery; on a half-open LISTEN connection by at most 3 s plus that delivery; and while LISTEN is down nothing is trusted and the store is read.

Settlement waits are signalled: a room's record closes an idle signal once it counts no update its persistence has not taken and no append that has not landed, and the waits for a room's updates and appends, the re-arm of a settlement whose wait ran out, and Shutdown's drain wake on it rather than polling. A settlement during shutdown waits for the document's lock at most 100 ms, in one statement under `lock_timeout`. A version's changed ask and comment anchors are written with one update per table and one batch of events.

A document socket's admission reads the issue and the caller once per open and hands both to ygo's authorization of the same request. The room and connection caps are counters (`DISPATCH_MAX_LIVE_ROOMS` and `DISPATCH_MAX_DOC_CONNECTIONS`, 1,000 each by default), a warm room's schema verdict is the one its replica observer recorded, and a cold open renders its document once. The cold reads' cache holds one sixteenth of the Go memory limit, at least 64 MiB and at most 1 GiB, and 256 MiB when no limit is set. The room figures (resident rooms, connections, pending updates, append batches, updates and round trips, the batch-size histogram, the room-appends and rooms pools, the read cache, the head tracker, the evictor's queries and the settlement wake-ups) are the expvar variable `dispatch_rooms` on the profiler port's `/debug/vars`.
