The cross-package automation no single package owns: the pull-request and main gate, the PR-title check, per-package semantic-release for the TypeScript packages and the Envoy listener, the path-scoped Envoy and contracts build, and the worker-image build that publishes `ghcr.io/sjawhar/legion-worker` by digest for the Kubernetes runtime to pin. `.github/scripts` holds the version-bump and release-push scripts those workflows call, with their own shell test. Every workflow's Postgres service is the pgvector-on-PostgreSQL-18 image Envoy's `internal/testpg` pins, by the same digest, and `envoy-go` prints the server's version and pgvector's before its tests; the exceptions are three jobs that run on production's Postgres 16, whose Cloud SQL instance holds Dispatch's, the secrets broker's and the Legion daemon's databases, until dispatch://INFRA-572: `dispatch-go-postgres-16` (Dispatch's Go suite) and `broker-go-postgres-16` (the broker's Go suite and its two-process tests) in the Envoy and contracts build, and `daemon-go-postgres-16` (the daemon's Go suite) in the pull-request and main gate. dispatch://INFRA-573 removes all three after INFRA-572.
