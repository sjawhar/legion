The sweep that asks GitHub to redeliver the App webhook's failed deliveries, which GitHub never redelivers itself. At start and then every two minutes, each wait the interval plus up to a tenth again drawn afresh every period (`duty.Jittered`), under the `webhook-redelivery` lease (`duty.Exclusive`, once per period across the processes), it lists the webhook's failed attempts as the App (`dispatch-auth`), judges each delivery by its GUID, and requests a redelivery of each the listener answered 5xx or GitHub could not complete, on a doubling backoff (2, 4, 8, 16, 32, then 60 minutes), until GitHub records an `OK` attempt for it or 72 hours have passed since the first failed attempt the sweep saw. Its cursor, one record per delivery acted on, one index entry per open delivery — keyed `pending.<YYYYMMDD>.<HH>.<MM>.<GUID>` by the minute that delivery next needs a sweep — and the marker that says every open record has an entry live in the JetStream KV bucket `envoy_webhook_redelivery`, reached through the listener's bus client (`envoy-listener`); its rate-limit backoff lives in Postgres (`github_rate_limits`, scope `redelivery`). The running sweep reads from that index the deliveries due in its window, rather than every record the bucket holds, and lists the last hour and six minutes (`maxBackoff` plus three intervals), long enough that a delivery coming due still sees GitHub's answer to its last request in the listing. `envoy-dispatch redeliver-webhooks` runs the same sweep over a chosen window, reading every record in the bucket rather than the index.
