The Oh My Pi plugin every session loads (`@sjawhar/pi-envoy`): Envoy subscriptions and delivery, the `dispatch` command on every shell's `PATH`, the per-request context (the `dispatch-first` skill at the head of every model request, and at the tail of each main-loop request, never of a side turn, the handoff prompt or a remote compaction's request, what people wrote to the session in the last 72 hours, read from Dispatch's `GET /api/v1/agents/{session_id}/answers` through `@legion/envoy-client/session-answers`, a subagent reading its parent's), and the five skills every session with Dispatch reads (the Dispatch and Envoy skills, and `asd-ste100`). It publishes the in-process interface in `pi-shared` that the Legion plugin claims its roles through. It takes the session's messages from the listener's relay of the session's own durable consumer, asked for at every registration and acknowledged once the session wrote the message (`packages/envoy/AGENTS.md`, "Session consumers"). It dials NATS with the client's shared connection options (a ping every 10 s, so a frozen path is left within 30 s), runs the core path's catch-up of missed messages (`POST /v1/replay`) only on a listener that answers `core`, and registers as `plugin` `@sjawhar/pi-envoy` with its package version as `plugin_version`.
