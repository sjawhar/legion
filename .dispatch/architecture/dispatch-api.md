Every HTTP route body: issues, artifacts, asks, comments, messages, projects, and the assignee, rank, label and component-attachment writes. The largest package in the server (87 files) and the one every Dispatch feature touches. It resolves an agent's actor through the listener's OIDC and routing packages, hence the edge to `envoy-listener`. The edge to `contracts` is the wire contract rather than a Go import: `contracts/src/dispatch-api.ts` declares the response shapes these handlers produce and the web app consumes, and code generation covers only the event envelope, so there is no Go type to import.

The live paths hold bounded state per reader. The event stream relay (`event_stream_relay.go`) is the process's one reader of the event log: its first subscriber starts a generation that listens on `dispatch_events` and reads each commit once, encoding each event's SSE frame once for every subscriber, and the cancel of its last subscriber stops it at once, so a process nobody subscribes to holds no LISTEN connection and reads nothing. `GET /api/v1/events` subscribes to it. A resume (`since=` or `Last-Event-ID`) at or below the relay's head is replayed at most 256 events whose frames total at most 1 MiB, counted by rows; past either bound it is replayed nothing. `X-Dispatch-Event-Replay: complete` is set only when the replay is everything the client lacks, which includes a resume ahead of this process's relay but within the log; a client that reconnects without it refetches what it shows. A connection whose 64-slot subscription overflows catches up from the log within the same bounds and stays open; past them, or when the relay's generation ends (an event log reset, the test hook, shutdown), its response ends. The delivery hub takes its events from the relay's feed (1,024 slots) and itself listens only on `delivery_changes` and `delivery_freshness`; it also stops with its last view, a closed feed makes it reload the open P0 issues and recompute every view, and an event that arrives during a view's read recomputes that view again. A comment's mentions are resolved in its request, up to 8 lookups at once, each bounded at 250 ms; the attempts commit pending with the comment, and the response returns them pending. The mention sender (`comment_mention_sender.go`) sends them after the response on one FIFO lane per recipient (the pinned session, else the target), 8 lanes at once and at most 4,096 sends queued; once shutdown starts it starts no queued send and gives the sends in flight 10 s, and a recovery pass at startup, and after an overflowed queue drains, resumes pending attempts created in the last hour whose claim is unset or lapsed. An agent-stream viewer (`agent_stream.go`) queues at most 8 MiB of frames; past that its stream ends.
